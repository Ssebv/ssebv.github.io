About

I build and run the systems a business actually depends on — ERP, inventory, accounting and electronic invoicing across four companies in three countries. My work sits where correctness of financial data and continuity of service are not features but the whole job.

Most recently I led a full ERP platform migration (Odoo 14 → 19) end to end, with no data loss, and built an automated monitoring system that reconciles inventory against the general ledger and surfaces failures before users notice them. I work mainly in Python, SQL and PostgreSQL, with regulated electronic invoicing for the tax authorities of Chile, Colombia and Peru.

I also build applications on large language models in Python and TypeScript, from shopping assistants to document extraction and RAG, and I measure them instead of assuming they work.

How I Organize Development

I split systems into clear layers, test them at several levels and release in stages, with monitoring in production. The full page explains the process and the architecture of five real systems, with the reasons behind each decision.

  2. Short branchesTests after each block
